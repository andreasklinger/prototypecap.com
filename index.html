With Fund I + Fund II we invested globally. About 1/3 Bay Area, 1/3 US, a good part in Europe and a lot in India.

But over the last years it became clear to us that we need to focus on Europe.

Due to its fragmented nature Europe is falling behind more and more against larger blocks like China or the US.
Additionally Europe sold way too much of its core industries to Asia – eg in precision manufacturing.

We believe Europe has one or maximum two decades to fix this situation.

We combine media, policy and investment to create a well functioning pan-European startup ecosystem and identity.
